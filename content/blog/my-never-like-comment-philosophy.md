## How do likes compare and why I also refrain from doing them

Turning off the engine review after losses. Watching a computer tell me my hopeful queen sac was "-6.2, blunder" sucked the joy out of the game faster than anything else I tried. Now I just glance at the result, maybe wince once, and queue up the next game.

## the actual appeal

There's something nice about a game where the entire world is 64 squares and the only thing that matters for the next ten minutes is whether that knight fork is real. It's the closest thing I have to meditation that also lets me occasionally feel like a genius for four moves in a row before immediately blundering my rook.
